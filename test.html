02 / About

A different way
of seeing yourself.

I work at the intersection of biology, psychology, and behaviour.

After years working closely with high-performing individuals, I noticed something nobody talks about — the most successful people often have the most fragile relationships. The thing they've optimised least is the thing that ends up shaping their lives the most.

So I focused on it.

I help people understand the patterns behind their choices and desires — so they can show up boldly, build better connections, and actually follow through on what they want.

This isn't dating advice. It's a way of seeing yourself clearly enough to stop repeating yourself.
